Privacy Policy

At Uptrail, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our learning management platform.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, enroll in courses, or contact us for support. This may include:

  • Name and contact information
  • Account credentials
  • Payment information
  • Course progress and performance data

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain our services
  • Process transactions and send notifications
  • Improve our platform and user experience
  • Communicate with you about our services

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.
