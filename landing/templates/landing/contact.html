Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enroll in a course?

Simply browse our course catalog, select the course you're interested in, and click "Enroll Now". You'll need to create an account and complete the payment process for paid courses.

Are there any free courses available?

Yes! We offer several free courses to help you get started. Look for courses marked as "Free" in our course catalog.

Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied?

Yes, we offer a 7-day money-back guarantee. Please check our Refund Policy for complete details.

How long do I have access to a course?

Once you enroll in a course, you have lifetime access to all course materials, including any future updates.

Do you provide certificates?

Yes, you'll receive a certificate of completion after successfully finishing a course. Certificates can be downloaded from your dashboard.
